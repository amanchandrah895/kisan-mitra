# Improved wheat cultivation practices (Central India)

The Central Zone covers Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Chhattisgarh, the Kota and Udaipur divisions of Rajasthan and the Jhansi division of Uttar Pradesh.

## Sowing time and number of irrigations
- Timely sown with restricted irrigation: 20 October to 10 November, 1 to 2 irrigations.
- Timely sown irrigated: 10 to 25 November, 3 to 5 irrigations.
- Late sown irrigated: 1 December to 5 January, 4 to 5 irrigations.
Varieties recommended for the zone include HI 1500 (Amrita), HI 1544 (Purna) and HI 1633 (Pusa Vani).

## Seed rate, seed treatment and spacing
Use 100 kg seed per hectare for bread wheat and 125 kg per hectare for durum wheat when sown on time; increase the seed rate by 25% for late sowing. A thumb rule: the 1000-seed weight in grams equals the seed rate in kg per acre. Treat seed with carboxin 1.25 g per kg, fipronil 6 g per kg or chlorpyriphos 8 ml per kg. Keep 20 cm between rows; fertilizer may be placed 2 to 3 cm deeper than the seed.

## Fertilizer (N:P:K kg per hectare)
Apply nitrogen, phosphorus and potash in a 4:2:1 ratio along with 10 tonnes of farmyard manure per hectare.
- Restricted irrigation: 80:40:20 (urea 180 kg, SSP 250 kg, MOP 30 kg).
- Timely sown irrigated bread wheat: 120:60:30 (urea 260 kg, SSP 375 kg, MOP 50 kg).
- Durum wheat: 140:70:35 (urea 300 kg, SSP 440 kg, MOP 60 kg).
- Late sown: 100:50:25 (urea 220 kg, SSP 310 kg, MOP 42 kg).

## Irrigation schedule
If water is plentiful, irrigate at 20 to 25 day intervals. With only one irrigation, give it at 35 to 45 days after sowing. With two irrigations, give them at 35-40 and 70-80 days after sowing. With three irrigations, give them at 20-25, 50-55 and 80-85 days after sowing.

## Weed control
Hoe at 15-20 and 35-40 days after sowing. Pre-emergence: pendimethalin 1.0 kg per hectare. Post-emergence at 30 to 45 days after sowing: for broadleaf weeds 2,4-D at 0.60-0.75 kg per hectare, metsulfuron methyl 4 g per hectare or halauxifen methyl 12.76 g per hectare; for narrow-leaf weeds clodinafop propargyl 60 g per hectare or sulfosulfuron 25 g per hectare. Metribuzin 250 g per hectare controls both types.

## Harvest and storage
Major diseases include stem rust, leaf rust, Karnal bunt and loose smut; termites and aphids are common pests. Store grain at about 12% moisture.
