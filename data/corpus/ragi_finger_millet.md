# Ragi (finger millet, Eleusine coracana)

## Seasons and varieties
- Irrigated, Margazhi pattam (December-January): CO 9, CO 13, CO (Ra) 14, TRY 1.
- Irrigated, Chithirai pattam (April-May): CO 9, CO 13, CO (Ra) 14.
- Rainfed, Adi pattam (June-July): Paiyur 1, CO 13, CO (Ra) 14, Paiyur 2.
- Rainfed, Purattasi pattam (September-October): Paiyur 1, CO 13, CO (Ra) 14.
CO 9 matures in 100-105 days and yields about 4500 kg per hectare under irrigation and 3100 kg rainfed. CO 13 matures in 95-100 days. CO (Ra) 14 matures in 105-110 days. Paiyur 1 matures in 115-120 days.

## Nursery for transplanted ragi
A nursery of 12.5 cents (500 square metres) is enough for one hectare. Mix 37.5 kg superphosphate with 500 kg farmyard manure and spread it. Make raised beds of 3 m x 1.5 m. Treat seed with Azospirillum and phosphobacteria (600 g each per hectare) or Azophos (1200 g), and with Thiram 4 g, Captan 4 g or Carbendazim 2 g per kg of seed. Broadcast 5 kg of treated seed in shallow rills not deeper than 1 cm. Seedlings are pulled on days 17 to 20.

## Transplanting and spacing
Transplant 2 seedlings per hill, 3 cm deep, when they are 18 to 20 days old. Spacing is 30 x 10 cm for the transplanted crop and 22.5 x 10 cm for direct sowing. Dip the roots for 15 to 30 minutes in a solution of Azospirillum and phosphobacteria (1000 g each) in 40 litres of water.

## Nutrient management (irrigated)
Apply 12.5 tonnes per hectare of farmyard manure or compost. Follow soil-test based doses where available; otherwise the blanket dose is 60 kg N, 30 kg P2O5 and 30 kg K2O per hectare. Apply half the nitrogen and all the phosphorus as basal before planting. Potassium may be skipped where soil potassium is 310 kg per hectare. Apply 12.5 kg micronutrient mixture mixed with sand to make 50 kg per hectare.

## Rainfed ragi
Rainfed ragi needs about 450-500 mm rainfall. Fertilizer dose is 40:20:20 kg N:P:K per hectare, with full P and K at sowing. Seed rate is 15-20 kg per hectare in rows 22.5-30 cm apart.

## Weed management
Pre-emergence oxyfluorfen 0.05 kg a.i. per hectare on the 3rd day after sowing followed by one hand weeding at 20 days, or hand weeding on the 10th and 20th day after transplanting.

## Pests and harvest
For root aphids, drench dimethoate 3 ml per litre around infested plants. Harvest in two stages: first cut the earheads when the main earhead and 50% of earheads turn brown, and cut all remaining heads seven days later. Thresh at 15% grain moisture and store grain at 10% moisture.
