# PMFBY crop insurance

## Premium paid by the farmer
Under Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana, the farmer pays a maximum premium of:
- 2% of the sum insured for Kharif crops;
- 1.5% of the sum insured for Rabi food crops and oilseeds;
- 5% of the sum insured for commercial and horticultural crops.
The rest of the premium is shared 50:50 by the Centre and the state, or 90:10 for the North Eastern states from Kharif 2020.

## Risks covered
The scheme covers crop loss from pre-sowing to post-harvest. Localized risks covered include hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloud burst and natural fire. Post-harvest losses caused by cyclone, cyclonic or unseasonal rain and hailstorm are covered for up to 14 days after harvest, when the crop is kept in the field to dry. Damage by wild animals is an optional add-on that a state may choose to offer at its own cost.

## Reporting crop loss
For localized calamities and post-harvest losses, the farmer must inform the loss within 72 hours of the event (earlier it was 48 hours). The loss can be reported to the insurance company's local office, the bank, the state agriculture department, the crop insurance web portal or app, or the toll-free number. For area-wide losses assessed through crop-cutting yield data at the end of the season, the farmer does not need to inform individually.

## Delayed claims
If the insurance company delays settlement beyond two months of the cut-off date, it must pay 12% interest to the farmer.
