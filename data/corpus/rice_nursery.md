# Rice wet nursery and seed treatment

## Nursery area
A wet nursery of 20 cents (800 square metres) is needed to raise seedlings for one hectare of main field. Select a site close to a water source.

## Seed rate by duration
The seed rate depends on the duration of the variety: 30 kg for long-duration varieties, 40 kg for medium-duration varieties, 60 kg for short-duration varieties and 20 kg for hybrids, for planting one hectare.

## Wet seed treatment with fungicide
Soak the seed in a solution of carbendazim, pyroquilon or tricyclazole at 2 g per litre of water for every 1 kg of seed. Soak for 10 hours and then drain the water. This wet seed treatment protects the seedlings from blast and other seedling diseases for up to 40 days and is better than dry seed treatment. If the seed is to be sown immediately, keep the soaked seed in a gunny bag in the dark, covered, for 24 hours so that it sprouts.

## Biological seed treatment
Bacillus subtilis (talc formulation) can be used at 10 g per kg of seed, soaked overnight in 1 litre of water; decant the water, allow sprouting for 24 hours and then sow. Biofertilizers can be used as seed soak: Azospirillum 3 packets (600 g/ha) plus phosphobacteria 3 packets (600 g/ha), or Azophos 6 packets (1200 g/ha), mixed in water and used to soak the seed overnight. Biocontrol agents are compatible with biofertilizers and both can be mixed for soaking, but fungicides and biocontrol agents are not compatible and should not be mixed.

## Preparing the seedbed
Form beds 2.5 m wide with channels of 30 cm around them. Bed length can be 8 to 10 m depending on soil type and slope. Raise the bed by spreading channel mud on it, and level the surface so that water drains into the channel. Sow the sprouted seeds evenly while a thin film of water is maintained on the nursery.

## Hybrid rice nursery (TNAU hybrid rice package)
For hybrid rice the seed rate is 20 kg per hectare. Apply DAP as basal at 2 kg per cent (100 square metres) of nursery. Sparse sowing at 1 kg seed per cent of nursery gives robust seedlings with 1 to 2 tillers each at planting time. On heavy soils apply 4 kg gypsum per cent of nursery 10 days before pulling the seedlings. Seedlings of 20 to 25 days age are used for planting.
