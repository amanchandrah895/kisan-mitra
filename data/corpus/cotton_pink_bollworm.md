# Pink bollworm (PBW) management in cotton

## Monitoring with pheromone traps
Start pheromone trap monitoring 45 days after sowing, using 5 traps per hectare. The economic threshold level (ETL) is 8 moths per trap per night for 3 consecutive days. Pheromone traps should also be placed near ginneries, godowns and market yards to catch moths after the season.

## Economic threshold levels
Take action when any one of these is reached:
- 8 moths per pheromone trap for 3 consecutive days;
- 10% damaged flowers (rosette flowers) at the squaring and flowering stage;
- 10% damaged green bolls, that is at least 2 out of 20 randomly plucked green bolls with larvae or exit holes.

## Cultural practices
- Terminate the crop by December or mid-January in central and south India and by October in north India; do not extend the crop beyond January.
- Rotate crops to break the PBW life cycle.
- Sow synchronously and prefer early-maturing, short-duration Bt hybrids or varieties.
- Pick clean and infested cotton separately and destroy infested cotton; do not store it in godowns.
- Clear residual stalks and partly opened bolls after the season.

## Botanical and biological control
Spray neem seed kernel extract 5% plus neem oil 50 ml plus detergent or soap 10 g in 10 litres of water at 50 to 60 days after sowing. Release Trichogramma bactrae egg parasitoids at 60,000 per acre, three times at weekly intervals, between 90 and 120 days after sowing, and avoid insecticide sprays for at least 10 days after each release.

## Chemical control (only after ETL)
Spray only when the pest crosses the ETL. Avoid synthetic pyrethroids before November and never use insecticide mixtures or tank mixes, because they cause whitefly and aphid outbreaks. Do not use extremely hazardous (red triangle) or highly hazardous (yellow triangle) insecticides, which kill beneficial insects. Always follow label claims.

## Refuge
Refuge-in-bag seed (Bt seed mixed with 5-10% non-Bt seed) delays resistance and should be used. Seed companies are advised to promote hybrids that mature in less than 180 days.
