# PM-KISAN income support scheme

## Benefit
PM-KISAN is a Central Sector Scheme that gives income support to landholding farmer families. Each eligible family receives Rs 6,000 per year, paid in three equal instalments of Rs 2,000 every four months, directly into the bank account (direct benefit transfer). The scheme took effect from 1 December 2018.

## Who is a family
A family means husband, wife and minor children. Land held by the family is pooled and only Rs 6,000 per year is paid per family.

## Eligibility
Families owning cultivable land as per state land records are eligible. Persons who do not have land in their own name are not eligible.

## Who is NOT eligible (exclusions)
- Institutional landholders.
- Families in which any member is a present or former holder of a constitutional post, a present or former minister, MP, MLA, mayor or chairperson of a district panchayat.
- Serving or retired officers and employees of central or state government, PSUs and autonomous bodies, and regular employees of local bodies (Multi Tasking Staff, Class IV and Group D employees are not excluded on this ground).
- Retired pensioners getting a monthly pension of Rs 10,000 or more (except Multi Tasking Staff, Class IV and Group D).
- Anyone who paid income tax in the last assessment year.
- Registered professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants and architects who are practising.

## Information needed for registration
Name, age, gender, category (SC/ST), Aadhaar number, bank account number and IFSC code; a mobile number is optional but advised. Aadhaar is mandatory and no benefit is paid without bank account details.

## Lists and grievances
Beneficiary lists are displayed at the panchayat. A farmer left out of the list can apply to the District Level Grievance Redressal Monitoring Committee. Wrong declarations lead to recovery of the benefit and penal action.
