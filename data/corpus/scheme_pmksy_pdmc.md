# Per Drop More Crop (micro irrigation)

## About the scheme
The Per Drop More Crop (PDMC) component of Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) has been implemented by the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare since 2015-16 in all states. It improves water use efficiency on farms through micro irrigation, mainly drip irrigation and sprinkler irrigation systems.

## Subsidy for drip and sprinkler systems
Financial assistance is given at 55% of the indicative unit cost for small and marginal farmers and 45% for other farmers. Several states give an additional top-up subsidy to reduce the farmer's share further.

## Micro Irrigation Fund
A Micro Irrigation Fund has been created with NABARD to help states expand micro irrigation coverage, take up special projects and give incentives beyond PDMC.

## Coverage and support
About 137.8 lakh hectares had been brought under micro irrigation by February 2022. ICAR, through Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), conducts trainings and field demonstrations on micro irrigation.
