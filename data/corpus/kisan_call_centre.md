# Kisan Call Centre

## Helpline number and timings
Farmers can call the Kisan Call Centre on the toll-free number 1800-180-1551 from any mobile or landline network. The service is available from 6:00 am to 10:00 pm on all seven days of the week. The scheme was launched on 21 January 2004.

## Languages
Farmers' questions are answered in 22 local languages.

## Who answers
Calls are answered by Farm Tele Advisors, who are graduates or postgraduates in agriculture or allied subjects and are fluent in the local language. Questions that they cannot answer are passed on through call conferencing to higher-level experts: subject matter specialists of state agriculture departments, ICAR institutes and state agricultural universities.

## When to call (Kisan Mitra editorial note, not from the source)
Call the Kisan Call Centre or visit the nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) for questions about a crop, pest, disease, livestock or scheme that are not answered here, or when the problem is serious, such as sudden crop damage or suspected pesticide poisoning (in which case also seek medical help immediately).
