# System of Rice Intensification (SRI)

## Season and varieties
SRI is more suitable for the dry season with assured irrigation. Establishment is difficult during heavy rains such as the north-east monsoon in Tamil Nadu. Hybrids and varieties with heavy tillering are preferred.

## Seed rate and mat nursery
Use 7 to 8 kg of seed per hectare for a single seedling per hill, or 12 to 15 kg where two seedlings per hill are needed because establishment is difficult. A mat nursery of 100 square metres is enough for one hectare. Spread a plastic sheet or used polythene on a shallow raised bed near the water source. Prepare 4 cubic metres of soil mix for 100 square metres: 70% soil, 20% well-decomposed pressmud, biogas slurry or farmyard manure, and 10% rice hull, with 1.5 kg powdered DAP or 2 kg of 17-17-17 NPK. Use a frame 0.5 m long, 1 m wide and 4 cm deep.

## Pre-germination and sowing
Soak the seed for 24 hours, drain and incubate for 24 hours, and sow when the radicle is 2 to 3 mm long. Sow 90 to 100 g of pre-germinated seed per square metre and cover with 5 mm of dry soil. Water with a rose can two or three times a day as needed, protect from heavy rain for the first 5 days, keep a thin film of water from day 6, and drain 2 days before lifting. If growth is slow, spray 0.5% urea plus 0.5% zinc sulphate at 8 to 10 days after sowing.

## Transplanting and spacing
Seedling mats are lifted at 15 days. Transplant one or two 15-day-old seedlings per hill within 30 minutes of uprooting, in a square pattern of 22.5 x 22.5 cm (9 x 9 inches). Fill gaps between the 7th and 10th day after transplanting.

## Weed management
Use a rotary weeder or cono weeder at 7 to 10 day intervals starting 10 to 15 days after planting, in both directions. Manual weeding is still needed near the root zone.

## Nutrients
Nutrient management follows the transplanted rice recommendation, with the Leaf Colour Chart used to time nitrogen. Green manure and farmyard manure improve growth and yield in SRI.
