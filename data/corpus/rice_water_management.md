# Rice main-field water management

## Water depth after transplanting
About 2 cm of water is adequate at the time of transplanting, and a 2 cm depth should be maintained up to seven days after transplanting. After establishment, cyclic submergence of 5 cm water is the best practice and is continued through the crop. Standing water should be kept at 5 cm or less.

## When to irrigate
Irrigate after the ponded water disappears. On loamy soils irrigate 1 day after disappearance in summer or 3 days after in winter. On clay soils irrigate just before or immediately after disappearance in summer, or 1 to 2 days after in winter.

## Critical stages
Panicle initiation, booting, heading and flowering are critical stages; soil moisture must not fall below saturation during these stages. Continuous flooding of 5 cm or more during booting and maturity speeds up root decay and leaf senescence, delays heading and reduces the number of filled grains. The last irrigation may be given 15 days before harvest.

## Alternate wetting and drying (AWD) with a field water tube
Alternate wetting and drying irrigation is monitored with a field water tube: a plastic pipe 40 cm long and 15 cm in diameter. The bottom part is perforated with 0.5 cm holes and the top 15 cm is left solid; marks are made every 5 cm. One tube is needed per acre and is installed near the field bund. Safe AWD with 10 cm water depletion in light soils and 15 cm depletion in heavy soils improves water use efficiency.

## Saving water
Good puddling and levelling reduce water requirement. A tractor-drawn cage wheel reduces percolation and can save up to 20% water. Keep 2.5 cm of water over the puddle while green manure decomposes: at least 7 days for sunnhemp and 15 days for Kolinchi (Tephrosia purpurea).

## Field precautions
Make plots of 25 to 50 cents depending on the water source, avoid field-to-field irrigation, and form a small bund 30 to 45 cm inside the main bund. In waterlogged fields open drains about 60 cm deep and 45 cm wide. Where water is short in double-cropped wetlands, groundnut or a pulse can be raised instead of Kuruvai rice.

## SRI water management
In the System of Rice Intensification (SRI), irrigate only to moisten the soil for the first 10 days. After hairline cracks develop, irrigate to a maximum depth of 2.5 cm until panicle initiation.
