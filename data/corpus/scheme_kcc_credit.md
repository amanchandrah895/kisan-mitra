# Kisan Credit Card and interest subvention

## What KCC offers
The Kisan Credit Card (KCC), introduced in 1998, gives farmers short-term credit for crop cultivation and related needs. It was extended to animal husbandry and fisheries in 2019.

## Interest rate and subvention
Under the Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS), short-term crop loans up to Rs 3 lakh through KCC are given at a concessional interest rate of 7% per year. Lending banks receive 1.5% interest subvention. Farmers who repay on time get a Prompt Repayment Incentive of 3%, so the effective interest rate becomes 4% per year. For loans taken only for animal husbandry or fisheries, the benefit applies to loans up to Rs 2 lakh. The Union Cabinet approved continuation of MISS for 2025-26 on 28 May 2025; it is implemented through RBI and NABARD.

## Budget 2025-26 enhancement
The Union Budget 2025-26 announced raising the loan limit under the modified interest subvention scheme from Rs 3 lakh to Rs 5 lakh for loans taken through KCC.

## Collateral
KCC loans up to Rs 2 lakh are collateral-free.

## Scale
By 31 December 2024 there were operative KCC loans of about Rs 10.05 lakh crore benefiting 7.72 crore farmers.
